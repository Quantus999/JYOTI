Understanding Jyotish

Jyotish (Sanskrit: ज्योतिष) means "Science of Light" — the ancient Vedic system of understanding life through celestial patterns.

The 70/30 Principle

~70% of life reflects Prarabdha Karma (patterns already in motion), while ~30% remains Kriyaman Karma (your response in this moment). The chart illuminates the weather; you choose how to dress.
